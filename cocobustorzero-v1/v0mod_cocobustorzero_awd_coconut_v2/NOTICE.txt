This is an independent source-part library, not an assembled printer or an
installation configuration. Original nonzero-area STL triangles and winding
are preserved. Unit face normals are recomputed. The right air duct and belted-Z
bed mount are withheld because of unresolved original mesh topology defects.
Built-in supports have not been identified or removed. No hardware is added.

The author's README credits VoronDesign, Coconut, LittleTimTim,
MathematicalPotato and Kl3pt, and cites 247printing as inspiration.
Current Coconut V2 is kept separate from TimTim AWD and archived Coconut V1.
The unfinished Superwide Stance exports are excluded.

Corresponding source files and their exact URLs/hashes: SOURCE_FILES.json.
Reproducible conversion: scripts/convert_cocobustor_sources.py in
https://github.com/Psych0h3ad/3d-print-rig
